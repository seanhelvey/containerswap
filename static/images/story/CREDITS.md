attribution; it's here anyway.

  photo of containers the owner actually bought and emptied, not a stock image

  Third choice for this spot: a Little Free Pantry (too similar to a Little Free
  Library considered next, both just "box on a post") and a zero-waste shop

  in soil, generic enough to represent any partner without over-specifying which
  kind. The homepage no longer carries an image for this section at all — text-only,
  like the privacy section already was.
